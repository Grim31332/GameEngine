Operation Cardboard Shadow

JROTC Stealth Training Simulation (Fictional)

This is a quest-driven first-person stealth mission in Washington, D.C. Every person is represented as a cardboard cutout for a clearly fictional classroom demo.

Briefing

  1. Infiltrate the White House training set without being detected by guard cutouts.
  2. Retrieve the cardboard President cutout from the Oval Office set piece.
  3. Exfiltrate to the extraction zone at the National Mall with the cutout.

Controls: WASD move, ←→ turn, Shift to crouch-sneak.
